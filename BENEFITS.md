# GoreeCloud Vault — Benefits

GoreeCloud Vault is intended to provide a self-hosted, privacy-first authority for credentials, passkeys, authentication secrets, protected records, secure autofill material, and application secrets without creating competing secret stores across GoreeCloud.

Current Development value is limited to the verified service, security, governance, and design foundations. Credential storage, autofill, passkeys, TOTP, sharing, recovery, synchronization, and other user-facing benefits remain planned until implemented and accepted.
